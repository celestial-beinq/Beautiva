06 How it works

From first search to the chair.

Track a booking
Step 01 1 minute

Pick a craft & studio

Filter Hairdressers, Barbers, Nail Techs, Lash Artists, Makeup Artists, or Tattoo Artists by Lagos or Abuja area and starting Naira price.

Step 02 Instant site

Inspect their studio site

Open their generated site like /business/glamour-studio to view real photos, services, specialists, hours, and reviews.

Step 03 Paystack / Studio

Choose slot & settle

Select date, time, and optional specialist. Pay in full with Paystack, put down a 30% deposit, or pay at the studio where allowed.
