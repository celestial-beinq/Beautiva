02 Our story

Great studios shouldn’t need a web developer just to take a booking.

Across Lagos and Abuja, some of the sharpest braiders, barbers, nail techs, and bridal MUAs still manage appointments through buried Instagram DMs and screenshots of bank transfers.

Beautiva changes that in minutes. When a studio uploads its services, Naira prices, team, and photos, we generate a dedicated website—like beautiva.ng/business/your-studio—complete with live slot availability, Paystack checkout, and instant booking references.
